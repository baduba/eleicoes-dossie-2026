## Phase 2 (2026-10-28 and 2026-10-29)
- Pre-registration Addendum 4 written before any new analysis.
- **A1:** the "permutation within the state" envelope was rejected in all four years (the order of arrival within the state is not random in any election). Under the pre-registered criterion, the line comes out as excess; later analysis (cut from 10% of sections) shows 2026/T2 below all placebos. Line kept with a design caveat.
- **A2:** the "by role" placebo (opponent) was inadequate. In 2022 the PT, which led the 1st round, lost absolute votes in 12.6% of sections; in 2026 it was the opponent (11.8%). Line kept with a caveat.
- **A3:** the ecological coefficients "opponent in T1 to PT in T2" are negative in every year; the difference between 2026 and 2022 does not measure conversion. With municipality fixed effects, the 2026 coefficient is the most negative. Line kept with a caveat; the identity-based accounting and the minimum net loss per section are published alongside.

- **Sources:** the official 2018/T1 totals I cited from memory were swapped (the correct ones, published by the TSE, match BWeb). Corrected in Phase 1.
