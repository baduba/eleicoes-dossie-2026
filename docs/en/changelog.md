- **A2:** the "by role" placebo (opponent) was inadequate. In 2022 the PT, which led the 1st round, lost absolute votes in 12.6% of sections; in 2026 it was the opponent (11.8%). Line kept with a caveat.

With municipality fixed effects, the 2026 coefficient is the most negative. Line kept with a caveat; the identity-based accounting and the minimum net loss per section are published alongside.
