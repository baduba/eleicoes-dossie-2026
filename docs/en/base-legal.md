# Legal and historical context

Only items with an indicated source are included. Press items are marked "(press)" and do not support conclusions.

## Statutes and regulations

- **Electoral Code (Law 4,737/1965), art. 144:** "Voting shall begin at 8 (eight) o'clock and shall end, except as provided in Art. 153, at 17 (seventeen) o'clock." **Art. 153:** at 17:00 the station board president "shall have numbered tickets handed out to all voters present", and voting continues in ticket order ([Planalto](https://www.planalto.gov.br/ccivil_03/leis/l4737compilado.htm)). This explains stations closing a few minutes after 17:00.
- **Elections Act (Law 9,504/1997):** art. 59, "Voting and the tabulation of votes shall be carried out by electronic means"; art. 59-A (printed vote record), declared unconstitutional under ADI 5,889; art. 61, the voting machine shall record each vote ensuring secrecy and inviolability; art. 62, only voters listed on the voting rolls may vote; art. 66, "Parties and coalitions may observe all phases of the voting and tabulation process" ([Planalto](https://www.planalto.gov.br/ccivil_03/leis/l9504.htm)).
- **TSE Resolution 23,673, of Dec 14, 2021** (oversight and audit procedures for the electronic voting system), amended by Resolutions 23,687/2022, 23,693/2022, 23,711/2022, 23,722/2023 and 23,728/2024 ([copy at TRE-CE](https://www.tre-ce.jus.br/eleicao/eleicoes-2024/arquivos-mlep-2024/resolucao-tse-n-o-23-673-de-14-de-dezembro-de-2021), [DJe](https://sintse.tse.jus.br/documentos/2021/Dez/23/diario-da-justica-eletronico-tse-edicao-eleitoral/resolucao-no-23-673-de-14-de-dezembro-de-2021-dispoe-sobre-os-procedimentos-de-fiscalizacao-e-audito), [Res. 23,728/2024](https://www.tse.jus.br/legislacao/compilada/res/2024/resolucao-no-23-728-de-27-de-fevereiro-de-2024)). Art. 3 lists the systems subject to observation, audit, digital signature, sealing and verification. The consolidated full text was not reviewed in this audit; the 2026-specific voting and tabulation resolutions could not be located and are therefore not cited.

## Printed vote

- **Supreme Court (STF), ADI 5,889** (rapporteur Justice Gilmar Mendes), granted in a virtual session held from Sep 4 to Sep 14, 2020: declared art. 59-A of Law 9,504/1997 (introduced by Law 13,165/2015) unconstitutional; grounds: risk to ballot secrecy and freedom of the vote ([publication](https://legis.senado.leg.br/norma/27395724/publicacao/32872544), [Migalhas](https://www.migalhas.com.br/quentes/333392/stf-impressao-de-voto-eletronico-e-inconstitucional)).
- **PEC 135/2019** (physical ballots verifiable by the voter): rejected on Aug 10, 2021 according to [Migalhas](https://www.migalhas.com.br/depeso/350377/voto-impresso-no-brasil--pec-135-19) (legal press; the date was not verified on the Chamber of Deputies website).

## Oct 25, 2026 extension: documented facts

All sources below are press sources. The text of the decision and the official TSE and STF statements could not be opened by automation (HTTP 403).

- Second-round voting was due to end at 17:00, Brasília time. Justice Cristiano Zanin (STF) ordered a two-hour extension, until 19:00, "in every polling station section in the country" ([Bahia Notícias](https://www.bahianoticias.com.br/noticia/322925-tse-orienta-tres-a-manter-secoes-eleitorais-abertas-ate-as-19h-por-ordem-do-stf)).
- Who requested it: the Coligação Brasil Pronto Pra Mais coalition, in an action against the municipalities of Salvador, Feira de Santana and Lauro de Freitas, citing a lack of public transport fleet and referring to the ADPF 1,013 precedent (Bahia Notícias; [Times Brasil](https://timesbrasil.com.br/brasil/decisao-2026-stf-determina-ao-tse-prorrogar-votacao-por-duas-horas-em-todo-o-brasil/)). The PL campaign also requested more time, citing rain and flooding (search synthesis, [Portal de Prefeitura](https://portaldeprefeitura.com.br/bastidores-da-politica/zanin-prorroga-votacao-segundo-turno-eleicoes-ate-as-19h/634826/)).
- Implementation: the TSE president, Justice Kassio Nunes Marques, instructed the TREs by official letter to keep stations still open running until 19:00. The decision was released at 17:03 and the letter went out at 17:49, after complaints from the TREs, which led to the closing of thousands of sections ([O Liberal](https://www.oliberal.com/politica/nunes-marques-comunica-tres-sobre-prorrogacao-da-votacao-ate-19h-1.1186733), Bahia Notícias).
- Tabulation: disclosure began at the scheduled time, was interrupted when 0.25% of sections had been tallied, and resumed after 19:00; votes from machines already closed would be tallied normally ([Times Brasil](https://timesbrasil.com.br/brasil/decisao-2026-tse-anuncia-retomada-da-totalizacao-e-divulgacao-dos-votos-apos-as-19h/)).
- **What the data show:** 98.2% of sections closed within 15 minutes of the nominal time; 6,714 (1.35%) closed 115 minutes or more later (RJ, SP, BA, PE, PB, SE); 99.6% of poll-receipts (BUs) reached the TSE after 19:00. This is consistent with the description that the extension applied where sections were still open.
